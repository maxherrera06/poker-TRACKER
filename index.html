Cards: rank 2–9 t j q k a + suit s h d c, e.g. ahkd 7c8c9d. Odds update as you type.
Log the hand: add a result where you acted, then press Enter: f fold · w40 won 40 · l25 lost 25 · x10 split (net +10). Examples: 7h2c f (folded preflop) · jsts 9h8d2c f (folded the flop) · ahkd 7c8c9d w60. Cards typed after the result are the runout you saw after folding: qsjd f 9c8c2h tells you if you'd have won.
Money in the hand: each time you bet or call, type $ and the amount (khjd $2 9s5c2d $4) or use + Put in. The blinds and bomb pot ante are counted for you. Then f or l with no amount charges the total, and w@30 means you collected a $30 pot, so it logs your profit (30 minus what you put in).
Blinds: tap SB or BB (or press [ / ]) when you're in the blinds. Folding with no amount charges the blind, and hands where you only put in the blind (including checking the BB and folding on the flop) don't count as hands you played. After an SB hand it moves to BB on its own.
Bomb pot: type b (or tap 💣) and the hand goes straight to the flop: b ahkd 7c8c9d w90. Fold on the flop with no amount and it counts the ante as lost.
Esc clears · - someone folded (+ undo) · the fold count resets every hand · you can also tap the cards and buttons below.
